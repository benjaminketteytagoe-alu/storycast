Dawn Chorus

The sound of an ordinary morning: a 45-second listening walk through the hour before sunrise.

SC A StoryCast original · Published 4 September 2026

  1. SoundNear silence. A soft, steady breeze

  2. SoundA single bird whistles a falling two-note call, several times

  3. SoundA second bird joins with a quick, rising trill

  4. SoundA distant bell rings three times

  5. SoundMany birds call over one another — the full dawn chorus

  6. SoundThe calls thin out and the breeze fades

About this story

Dawn Chorus is an audio-only story told entirely in sound. It starts in near silence and builds, layer by layer, into a full chorus of birdsong before fading back into the breeze.

If you can’t hear it, the timestamped transcript describes every sound in order.
