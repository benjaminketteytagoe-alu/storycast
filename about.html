flagOur mission

Ordinary lives, worth hearing

We’ve got the dreamers. We’ve got the doers. We’ve got the saints, the rebels, the believers, and the doubters. We see the world through a million different lenses. We vote differently, we love differently, we pray differently, and we play differently.

To anyone watching from the outside, it looked like the start of an ordinary, completely forgettable day. The world was wrapped in a sleepy, quiet gray. Front doors unlocked. Garage doors rolled up with a heavy metallic yawn. Keys jangled, coffee machines dripped, and thousands of shoes hit the pavement in a steady, synchronized march. If you looked up the word everyday in the dictionary, this was it. It meant routine. It meant standard issue. It meant the background noise of life. But then, the sun finally cracked over the horizon, and the gray began to shatter into a million bright, chaotic colors.

“A story isn’t finished until everyone can get to the end of it.”

What every story includes

  • closed_caption

    Captions

    But under the skin? We are running on the exact same fuel. Hope. Love. Connection. A desire to be seen.

  • description

    Transcripts

    Every story has an interactive transcript you can download: select a timestamp to jump to that moment.

  • record_voice_over

    Audio description

    On any video, turn on Audio description and your device reads out what happens on screen.

  • A skip link, a logical tab order, visible focus outlines and media shortcuts that only work inside the player.

  • contrast

    Contrast and themes

    All text meets at least 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark themes. Your system theme is used unless you pick one.

  • zoom_in

    Zoom and reduced motion

    Pages reflow to one column at 320px and 400% zoom, and animations switch off if your device asks for less motion.
